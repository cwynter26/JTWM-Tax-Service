QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked
Questions.

We know taxes can bring up a lot of questions. Here are a few answers to help you feel more prepared and confident.

What tax services does JTWM offer?

JTWM Tax Services LLC offers individual and business tax preparation, self-employed and 1099 tax services, tax amendments, tax planning, and tax consultations. We also provide in-person and mobile notary services.

Do you offer virtual appointments?

Yes! JTWM offers virtual and in-person appointments, giving you flexibility to choose the option that works best for your schedule and needs.

What documents should I bring to my tax appointment?

Documents may include a government-issued photo ID, Social Security numbers or other applicable taxpayer identification information, W-2s, 1099s, records of other income, and documents related to potential deductions or credits.

The exact documents needed depend on your situation. Jacqueline can help you determine what to prepare before your appointment.

Can you help correct a previously filed tax return?

Yes. JTWM offers tax amendment services. If you believe a previously filed return contains an error or needs updating, contact us to discuss your situation and the appropriate next steps.

Do you work with self-employed individuals?

Absolutely! We provide tax preparation services for freelancers, independent contractors, and self-employed professionals, including individuals who receive 1099 forms.

Do you offer mobile notary services?

Yes! Jacqueline offers in-person notarization and may be able to travel to your location.

Mobile appointments depend on your location and her availability. Please contact JTWM to discuss travel arrangements and applicable fees.

How do I schedule an appointment?

You can contact JTWM Tax Services LLC to request an appointment. Online scheduling will also be available once our booking system is ready.

Still have questions? We're happy to help.
