Privacy Policy.

JTWM Tax Services LLC

Effective Date: October 26, 2026

JTWM Tax Services LLC respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and handle information when you visit our website, contact us, or request an appointment.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry through our website, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if provided
  • The service you are interested in
  • Information included in your message

When online scheduling is available, our scheduling provider may collect appointment-related information, such as your name, contact details, selected appointment time, and responses to scheduling questions.

Our website and third-party providers may also process technical information, such as IP addresses and browser information, as necessary to operate their services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use information submitted through our website to:

  • Respond to inquiries
  • Communicate about our services
  • Arrange and manage appointments
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain and improve our website
  • Meet applicable legal obligations

We do not use the general contact form to collect tax returns, Social Security numbers, financial account details, or other sensitive tax documents.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Formspree to process general contact form submissions.

If online scheduling is enabled, we may use Calendly to manage appointment requests.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

4. Information Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Please do not submit Social Security numbers, tax documents, financial account details, or other sensitive information through our general contact form or appointment scheduling questions.

If sensitive documents are needed, we will provide separate instructions for an appropriate secure method of sharing them.

5. Information Sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate our website, respond to inquiries, or manage appointments.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law.

We do not sell personal information collected through our website.

6. Data Retention

We retain inquiry and appointment information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the services involved.

7. Your Privacy Questions

You may contact us to ask questions about information you have submitted through our website or to request its correction or deletion, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.

8. External Services and Links

Our website may contain links to third-party services.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of websites or services we do not control.

We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our website, services, or legal requirements.

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
